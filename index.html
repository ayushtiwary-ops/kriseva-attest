Source-to-field review / bounded demonstration

Show the evidence behind a reported field, and stop when the record disagrees.

KRISEVA ATTEST is a human-reviewed evidence workbench for one fictional reporting case. The demonstration compares candidate values, exposes conflict or missing support, and records a named human decision.

Demonstration method

Compare / Escalate / Record

  1. 01 / COMPARE

    Keep source and value together

    Three whitelisted fields resolve to supported, conflicting, or unsupported evidence states.

  2. 02 / ESCALATE

    Stop at conflict or absence

    Unequal values remain conflicting. Missing evidence produces an explicit abstention.

  3. 03 / RECORD

    Attribute the human decision

    The receipt retains candidates, source fingerprints, the reviewer, the reason, and unresolved items.

Operating boundary

What this demonstration is allowed to show.

01

Synthetic and deterministic

The case is fictional. The disclosed trace makes no live model call.

02

No filing

The prototype does not write or submit an official return.

03

No regulator connection

It is not connected to IFSCA systems or DRR interfaces.

04

Human decision required

Conflict remains unresolved until a named reviewer records a reason.

Founder-owned engineering and integration.

The repository, deterministic review logic, interface integration, verification, and publication gates remain founder-owned. No additional public team role is asserted on this surface.
