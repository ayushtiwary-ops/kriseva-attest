Source-to-field review / bounded demonstration

Show the evidence behind a reported field, and stop when the record disagrees.

KRISEVA ATTEST is a human-reviewed evidence workbench for one fictional reporting case. The demonstration compares candidate values, exposes conflict or missing support, and records a named human decision.

Built for compliance teams at fund management entities (FMEs) in GIFT IFSC assembling evidence for periodic IFSCA returns. Fields are illustrative of those reported in FME periodic returns under the IFSCA (Fund Management) Regulations, 2025.

Anticipated challenge / answered in advance

Hard questions, straight answers.

What is actually different here?

Not a feature list: an accountability contract. Every reported value carries its exact source, competing candidates stay visible instead of being resolved silently, a named human must give a reason before a conflict closes, and the evidence exports as a portable manifest. We call this a hypothesis, not a validated moat.

Why can't ChatGPT or Copilot do this?

A generic assistant answers a prompt once. It does not persist a source binding, retain conflicting candidates, force a named decision before export, or emit a tool-independent manifest. That is a workflow-state design, not a model-quality gap.

Does DRR make this redundant?

DRR is submission-side infrastructure; ATTEST sits upstream, proving which source supported a field before it reaches a DRR form. We claim no gap: equivalent source-region provenance from DRR or an administrator is a pre-registered kill condition for this wedge.

Where is the agentic AI?

Nowhere in the running code today, and the screens say so. What exists is the contract any agent must obey: propose a value with exact references, abstain when evidence is missing, and let only a named human decide. Putting a model inside that boundary is the eight-week build target.

Is human-in-the-loop an admission the AI failed?

No. In regulated reporting the accountable human is the requirement, not a fallback: someone signs under their own name. The design makes that decision explicit, attributed, reasoned, and separately confirmed under maker-checker review.

Why trust this team?

KRISEVA already ships evidence-first, human-review procurement software in Indian defense today. That process discipline transfers: source binding, human sign-off, an auditable trail, under a demanding audit culture. Fintech domain knowledge is what the residency provides; the claim discipline here is machine-enforced.
