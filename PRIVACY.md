# Privacy boundary

The committed demonstration uses a fictional entity, fictional source labels, fixed synthetic values, and fictional demo personas (Priya Nair and Rohan Mehta, both invented for the walkthrough). It is designed to work without collecting personal or regulated data.

## Current repository behavior

- No account, authentication, analytics, telemetry, form submission, or remote storage is present.
- Review state exists only in the current page session and reset restores the known fixture.
- The downloadable manifests contain the synthetic case, fictional source fingerprints, the fictional reviewer and officer persona names, a fixed reason, and fixed timestamps.
- The public hub contains no email address, phone number, date of birth, résumé, private filesystem path, or personal application receipt.

## Prohibited data

Do not add real identity documents, contact details, financial records, regulated records, customer documents, bank data, KYC data, credentials, secrets, browser captures, notifications, or private application material.

Any future permissioned test requires purpose limitation, minimization, access control, retention and deletion rules, incident handling, and legal review before data is introduced.
